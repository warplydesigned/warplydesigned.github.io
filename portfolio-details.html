Case Study

Warehouse robotics digital twin

A simulation-first rollout for autonomous material handling, integrating telemetry, safety workflows, and operator training inside a single digital twin.

Challenge

The client needed to validate automation workflows and safety logic across a fleet of robots without disrupting live operations or risking downtime.

Approach

Warply Designed built a real-time simulation environment, connected it to live telemetry streams, and deployed an operator dashboard to track system health and performance.

Outcome

The team reduced commissioning time, improved safety coverage, and delivered a repeatable workflow for future facilities.

Simulation stack

Unreal Engine environment, Three.js preview tools, and data replay automation.

Automation layer

Custom backend services for job scheduling, telemetry aggregation, and alerts.

Operator tools

Mobile-ready dashboards and training simulations for on-site teams.
